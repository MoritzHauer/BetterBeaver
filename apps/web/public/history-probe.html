History probe

Not the app — a bare page testing which kind of history entry survives a real back press on this device. Firefox for Android is reported to skip entries made with pushState when the user presses back, while still honouring them for history.back() — which is why the app's back button works and the system one does not.

The log is saved on this device and survives leaving the page. Do one test at a time, in order, and press back only once per test.

Already done automatically when this page loaded — exactly what the app does at boot. Press back once now, then come back here and read the log.
