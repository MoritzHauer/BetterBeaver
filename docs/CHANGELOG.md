## 0022 Scheduling ladder & review flow

designed 2026-08-05 (13-question owner grilling over a research pass on Anki/FSRS, Duolingo's half-life regression and the retrieval-practice literature) — the self-grade buttons were the trigger: `sm2.ts` gave Hard and Good the _identical_ next interval (same `quality >= 3` branch, pre-update ease), so Hard meant nothing. ## 0024 Exercise difficulty ladder
